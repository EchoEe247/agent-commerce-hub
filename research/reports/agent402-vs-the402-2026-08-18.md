### Verification Levels

The402 offers three verification tiers:
1. **unverified** — no identity check beyond wallet ownership
2. **email_verified** — email confirmation completed
3. **Government-ID verified** — third-party KYC via `/v1/verification/start` (available but **no providers have completed it** as of crawl date)

**ID requirements for buyers:** None. Browse and purchase via x402 with no account or API key.  
**ID requirements for providers:** Optional. Email verification is the most common; government-ID verification is available but unused by current providers.

### Competition Landscape

- **Provider concentration:** High. Full Chair Studio controls 39% of all listings (39/100).
- **New-provider dominance:** 100% of catalog services show `provider_is_new: true` in the API response, indicating either a data-display quirk or that most providers have not yet completed verified jobs.
- **Actual completed jobs:** Only 2 providers show non-zero completed jobs (Full Chair Studio: 12, Opportunity Signal: 2). Total catalog-reported completed jobs: 470, but this is inflated by Full Chair Studio's 12 jobs repeated across 39 listings.
- **Completion rates:** Average 49% across providers with data; many new providers show `null` completion rates.
- **Reputation scores:** All visible scores are 75 (the Beta prior for new providers), with confidence = 0. No provider has yet built a differentiated reputation track record.

### Opportunities Without Government-ID Verification

- **All 19 catalog providers and 49/67 total participants are unverified or email-verified only.**
- No KYC/government-ID is required to:
  - Browse the catalog
  - Purchase services (x402 payment IS authentication)
  - List services (free, no onboarding)
  - Bid on requests
  - Earn USDC
- Government-ID verification (`/v1/verification/start`) is available but confers no current competitive advantage because no buyer or request poster is filtering on it.
- **Bid ceiling scales with verification tier**, but with zero active requests, this is moot.
